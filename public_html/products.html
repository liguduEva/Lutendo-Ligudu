Our Sweet Treats

At Sweet Crumbs Pops Bakery, we make a variety of fresh treats for different occasions. Here are some of the things we offer.

Cakes

Our cakes are made for birthdays, celebrations and those days when you just feel like having something sweet. We can also create cakes for different themes and occasions.

Cupcakes

Our cupcakes are small, tasty and perfect for sharing. They can be decorated in different ways depending on the occasion.

Whole Bread

We also bake fresh whole bread that is great for breakfast, lunch or simply enjoying at home with your favourite spread.

Cake Pops

Cake pops are one of our fun treats. They are easy to enjoy and can be decorated with different colours and designs.

Sourdough Bread

Our sourdough bread is made for people who enjoy a fresh, homemade loaf with a delicious texture and flavour.
